A decision followed by a later fill

BLS check: enter after a positive original payroll-change release in the preceding 24 hours. SEC check: enter after an available symbol-specific Item 2.02 filing in the preceding 24 hours. Both exit after 20 held sessions. Neither uses sentiment or pre-release consensus.

Timing limits: revised SIP bars use assumed evening availability; FX is the reviewed lagged reference series. BLS uses a five-minute delivery assumption; SEC uses next-session-close availability.
